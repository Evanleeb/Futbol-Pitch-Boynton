Nothing in the way

The structural grid falls on the demising lines, so the bay is a true clear span. Pitch length is limited by the shell, not by a column you have to build around. Only the north strip is fixed: entry, office, storage and two changing rooms with showers.

Two party rooms, wherever you want them

Both rooms start off the entry so birthday traffic never crosses the turf, but they are dry rooms and you can put them anywhere in the suite. 462 square feet each, four tables, twenty four seats.

Change it and see it

Drag a pitch or a party room and the model rebuilds as you go. The fit check calls out overlaps, anything past the demising line, and any room you cannot reach from the entry without crossing turf.
